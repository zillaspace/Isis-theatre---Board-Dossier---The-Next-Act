Operating model after opening

The building runs through operators rather than Aspen Film staff. Operating profit pays the HOA and building costs, and any surplus returns to programming. There is no profit motive; the goal is a building that pays for itself and keeps prices down for the community.

Campaign process

  • Quiet phase only. No public launch.
  • Board members and friends of Aspen Film identify individual prospects and open conversations one on one.
  • A single tracker records who is speaking to whom and any potential donation amount. Mimi and Mark edit it; access stays narrow so no prospect hears from two people.
  • The campaign page goes to the full board first for alignment, with a short cover note from Mimi on the process above.
  • Gift levels and recognition are confirmed only in a written agreement, and the EC signs off the five circle hierarchy before any circle is discussed with a donor.
  • Each donor is asked for one Aspen Film story for their wall entry at the time of the gift, in their own words, so the wall fills as the campaign does.

The addition of a Cinema sign

A separate workstream. The brief for a sign shop: a simple, modern, weather resistant “Cinema” sign that projects off the building on a bar, never touches the historic sign, and is visible from the corner.

  • Ask two or three shops, including local ones, to propose the most reasonably priced solution that meets the brief. Let them design it.
  • Early pricing seen so far runs $4K to $9K. That is from one search, not a quote. Planning estimate
  • It cannot be self illuminated and must be lit by existing fixtures within the outdoor lighting code.

Before the deck goes out

  • The 1912 history claims are drawn from general knowledge. Confirm the opening year and the origin of the name with the Aspen Historical Society before public use.
  • The two filmmaker lines are short attributed paraphrases. The interlude is original and can be edited freely.
  • Every planning estimate is marked with a gold pill. Remove the pill only when a real quote or a board decision replaces the number.
